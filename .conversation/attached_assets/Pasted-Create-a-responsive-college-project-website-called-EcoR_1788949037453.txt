Create a responsive college project website called **EcoRoute AI**.

### Concept

Combine these two ideas:

**1. AI Traffic Emission Estimator**
Estimate CO₂ emissions for a route based on distance, transport mode, and traffic level.

**2. Multi-Modal Route Optimizer**
Allow users to compare:
🚗 Car | 🚌 Bus | 🚲 Bicycle | 🚶 Walking | 🚆 Public Transport

Compare:

* Travel time
* Cost
* Distance
* Estimated CO₂
* Overall score

The system recommends the best option based on the user's priorities.

### Required Pages

Create at least **5 functional pages**:

1. **Home** – Explain the environmental problem, target users and EcoRoute AI solution.
2. **Plan Route** – Enter From and To locations and calculate route options.
3. **Compare Routes** – Compare all transport modes in cards/table.
4. **Environmental Impact** – Show CO₂ comparison, savings and charts.
5. **About & References** – Explain the project, limitations, future scope and external references.

### Interactive Features

Include at least these 3:

1. **Route Calculator** – Enter locations and generate results.
2. **Priority Slider** – Fastest ↔ Greenest; change the recommended mode dynamically.
3. **Traffic Selector** – Low / Moderate / Heavy / Severe traffic; changing traffic should change car travel time and estimated CO₂.

### Emission Logic

Use simple configurable formulas:

**CO₂ = Distance × Emission Factor × Traffic Factor**

Use different estimated factors for car, bus, public transport, bicycle and walking.

Clearly label values as **estimated/demo values**, not exact real-world measurements.
