# EcoRoute AI 🌱

## The Website link : https://eco-route-ai--raymondmaijo.replit.app/

**Move with less impact.**

EcoRoute AI compares the fastest, cleanest, and average driving routes, then estimates how your chosen 4-wheeler or 2-wheeler affects CO₂ and NO₂ emissions.

> A drive is more than minutes. Two routes can reach the same destination while creating different fuel use and exhaust emissions. EcoRoute makes that tradeoff visible — without pretending a classroom model is live navigation.

## Why it exists

Distance, traffic, and stop-start driving affect what your vehicle emits. Most navigation apps optimize for time alone. EcoRoute adds the missing dimension: **emissions**. It surfaces the tradeoff between speed and pollution so you can make an informed choice at the moment of departure.

## Features

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 🗺️ **See 2 routes** | Compare Fastest and Cleanest route choices on an interactive Kochi map. |
| 🚗 **Choose your vehicle** | Use a realistic Indian car or bike model to personalize the estimate. |
| 💨 **Understand CO₂** | View estimated kilograms of CO₂ for each route and vehicle. |
| 🌫️ **Track NO₂ too** | See an indicative tailpipe NO₂ estimate in grams alongside CO₂. |

## Getting started

1. Pick your start and end locations on the Kochi map.
2. Select your vehicle type and model year.
3. Review the three route options and their respective CO₂ (kg) and NO₂ (g) footprints.
4. Choose the route that fits your priorities — time, emissions, or a balance of both.

## A note on accuracy

EcoRoute is an educational and planning aid. Its figures are **indicative estimates** modeled from distance, traffic conditions, and driving patterns — not real-time telemetry from your vehicle or live navigation data.
